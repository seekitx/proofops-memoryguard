THE SAFETY LOOP

Remembering is only half the job.
Recovery needs a reason.

01 / REMEMBER

Keep the risk.

Trusted risk persists in Sibyl across processes. Instruction-like external text is quarantined as data.

02 / CONTAIN

Stop the right work.

Risk blocks related tasks and their dependents. Unrelated tasks can continue under their own valid conditions.

03 / RECONSIDER

Earn a new decision.

Resolve each blocker with current evidence and a distinct reviewer role. Explicit reconsideration creates a new decision.
